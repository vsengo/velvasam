Mission

Velvasam Charity is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing support and assistance to underprivileged students, patients, and elderly individuals in the North and East Provinces of Sri Lanka. Our mission is to make a positive impact on the lives of those in need through various charitable initiatives and programs.


We believe that every individual deserves access to education, healthcare, and basic necessities, regardless of their socio-economic background. Through our efforts, we aim to empower individuals and communities, helping them overcome challenges and build a better future.
